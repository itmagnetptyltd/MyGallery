# MyGallery quick start

**For:** the person who uses MyGallery on their own Windows 11 PC\
**Applies to:** MyGallery 0.1.0

A photo on your PC becomes a Photo you can see, describe, download and delete.

## Before you begin

- MyGallery is running, in your web browser. See `README.md`, "Before you
  start" and "Run MyGallery".

## 1. Open the Upload popup

**To open the Upload popup:**

- In the header, select **Upload photos**. On an empty Gallery, this same
  control opens the popup.

**Result:** The Upload popup opens.

## 2. Choose your photos

**To choose your photos:**

- Select **Choose files**, then choose one or more photos on your PC.

**Result:** A preview of each chosen photo appears in the popup, confirming
your choice.

## 3. Upload them

**To upload your photos:**

1. Optional: In the **Description (optional)** field, enter a description.
2. Select **Upload**.

**Result:** A new Thumbnail card appears in your Gallery for each Photo you
uploaded, with the most recently uploaded first.

## What you just did

Each photo you uploaded is now a Photo, shown with a Thumbnail in your Gallery.
