# FB-0001 — Thumbnail cards on the home Gallery should be larger and redesigned

- **Received:** 2026-09-18
- **From:** KCB / DEV
- **Channel:** Chat
- **Anchors:** REQ-GAL-003
- **Triage:** proposed — preference
- **Sentiment:** negative

## What they said

> Thumbnail /card on hoem need to larger and design need to change in  agood way

## What we think it means

A reading, not a fact: the Gallery tiles feel too small, and the look of the
card (not only its size) should change. "in a good way" names no size, spacing
or style, so this is taste about the delivered Gallery, not a missed
acceptance criterion. REQ-GAL-003 requires a Thumbnail for every Photo, newest
first. It does not specify how large the card is or how it is styled.

If the ask grows into a new visual language for the whole Gallery (not just
larger tiles), that would be a variation rather than a preference. That is
for a human to decide.

## Resolution

Absorbed 2026-09-18, no charge. `/find-variation` split this into two
asks; both were `not-covered`. CHG-0001 (larger cards) and CHG-0002
(card design) moved REQ-GAL-003 v1 → v2.
