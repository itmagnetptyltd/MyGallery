# Plan — REQ-GAL-004@v3

Slice 3, Larger view. The v3 criteria come from FB-0006 (CHG-0014), clarified
by the answer of 2026-09-22: "in image preview will have two buttons "Save
Description" and "Close" buttons."

Gate: `close-slice.js --gate REQ-GAL-004` passed — slices 1 and 2 are Done.
Dependency REQ-GAL-003 is `verified` (v4, slice 2, which put Download and
Delete on every card).

## What must be true

c1-c6 are unchanged from v2 and already built and tested. They need their
pins moved to `@v3` after re-reading, not new code. 16 annotations across
five files.

1. **c1** Activating a Thumbnail shows that same Photo in the Larger view.
2. **c2** The Photo is rendered larger than its Thumbnail.
3. **c3** Escape closes the Larger view and shows the Gallery.
4. **c4** The close control closes it and shows the Gallery.
5. **c5** Closing returns the Gallery to where it was scrolled.
6. **c6** The close control is inside the panel.

Changed in v3:

7. **c7 — the panel's actions.** The Larger view's actions are a Save
   description button and a Close button, side by side. It shows no Download
   control and no Delete control. Today its row is Save description,
   Download, Delete.
8. **c8 — the Close button closes.** Activating the Close button hides the
   Larger view and shows the Gallery again, as the round close control does.

The round close control (×) at the top right stays: c4 and c6 still require a
close control inside the panel, and REQ-GAL-013@v3 c8 and REQ-GAL-019@v2 c4
style the Upload popup's close control after it.

## Approach

Markup, browser code and tests. The server, the API and the CSS rules are
untouched: the Close button reuses `.outline-button`, the look of Save
description, and `.larger-view-actions` already lays a row out centred with a
gap.

- `src/mygallery/templates/gallery.html` — in `.larger-view-actions`, remove
  the `download-photo` anchor and the `delete-photo` button, and add
  `<button type="button" class="outline-button"
  data-testid="larger-view-close-button">Close</button>` after Save
  description.
- `src/mygallery/static/app.js` — drop the `downloadPhoto` and `deletePhoto`
  lookups, the line that sets the download `href` when a Photo is opened, and
  the Larger view's Delete handler. The Close button gets the same handler as
  the × control: `largerView.close()`. The confirm handler no longer needs to
  close the Larger view, since only a card can ask now; `pendingDeletePhotoId`
  from slice 2 stays as the one source of which Photo is being deleted.

## What I am unsure about

- **"side by side".** Read as the same row, Save description first, as in the
  answer's order. The test checks equal top edges, not exact positions.
- **The × stays.** The answer names two buttons in the actions row and says
  nothing about the round control. It is kept because two other requirements
  depend on it; if the client wants it gone, that is a new change.
- **REQ-GAL-014 coverage dips until slice 7.** Its v1 tests go, and its v2
  tests arrive with slice 7. It is `agreed`, not `verified`, so no verified
  claim loses its evidence.
